🎓 The Anatomy Every Scam Shares

  • They pay you? No — you pay them. Legitimate employers never charge training, registration or equipment fees, and never pay by cheque you must "partially refund".
  • Urgency is the lockpick. "Act now, only 2 spots" exists to stop you doing the one thing that defeats every scam: pausing to verify.
  • Off-platform = off the record. Moving from a job site to WhatsApp/Telegram removes reporting, moderation and evidence in one step.
  • Verify backwards. Contact the real company through its official website — never through the phone number or link inside the suspicious message.
